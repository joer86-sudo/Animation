Why you're seeing this now

Please wait until 1 December to give.

Gifts made before GivingTuesday don't earn a share of the matching fund. For the next few weeks we're simply spreading the word, so that when the day comes, you're ready and your gift goes further.

Project Shelter builds safe, two-bedroom concrete homes together with families who could never build one alone: widows, people with disabilities, people who are bedridden and families who lost their home to disaster. Every home is built by many people giving a little.

On GivingTuesday only

Every gift earns a share of a $500,000 fund.

GlobalGiving adds it on top of what you give, and it all goes to the homes.

How the match works

GlobalGiving shares the fund across every eligible gift made that day.

It isn't dollar for dollar. When the day ends, GlobalGiving divides the $500,000 among all eligible GivingTuesday gifts on its platform, so every gift gets the same percentage on top.

Questions

Before you give

Why not give today?

You can, and we're grateful for every gift. But only gifts made on GivingTuesday earn a share of GlobalGiving's matching fund, so waiting until 1 December makes your gift go further.

Is it a one-to-one match?

No. The $500,000 is shared across all eligible GivingTuesday gifts on GlobalGiving in proportion to their size. Every eligible gift gets the same percentage on top.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

GlobalGiving is a US 501(c)(3) charity and sends a receipt for every gift, which is tax-deductible for US donors. Rules in other countries differ, so check what applies to you.

Is there a limit per donor, and do monthly gifts count?

GlobalGiving publishes the full eligibility rules for GivingTuesday, including any limit per donor and which kinds of gift count. We'll share the details here before 1 December.
