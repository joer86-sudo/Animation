What happens on December 1

GlobalGiving, the nonprofit crowdfunding platform that hosts our fundraiser, sets aside a $500,000 Incentive Fund for GivingTuesday. Every eligible donation made that day to a participating project, including ours, earns a share of it.

The match is proportional, not dollar for dollar. When the day ends, GlobalGiving divides the fund across all eligible gifts. Here's an example:

Questions

Why not just donate today?

You can, and we're grateful for any gift. But only donations made on GivingTuesday earn a share of GlobalGiving's matching fund, so waiting until December 1 makes your gift go further.

Is it a 1:1 match?

No. The $500,000 is shared across all eligible GivingTuesday donations on GlobalGiving in proportion to their size. Every eligible gift gets the same percentage bonus.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

GlobalGiving is a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit and sends you a receipt for your donation. Check with your tax adviser about your own situation.

Do monthly donations count?

GlobalGiving publishes the full eligibility rules for GivingTuesday, including limits per donor and which donation types count. We'll share the details here before December 1.

Matching funds are provided and administered by GlobalGiving. Terms are set by GlobalGiving and may change.
